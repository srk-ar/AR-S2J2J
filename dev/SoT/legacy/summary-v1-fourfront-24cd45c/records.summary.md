The dynamic analysis uses complete excitation time histories; the instrumental database therefore preserves the duration, cycle sequence, and frequency content of the measured vibrations as well as their peak amplitudes. The complete instrumental population provides the temporal shapes for the scenario realizations, while the subset with source-to-receptor distance and maximum instantaneous charge (MIC) characterizes the blasting conditions documented by the mining operation's contractor.

Particle velocity is the primary measurement. Each record is regularized on a uniform time axis and transformed under a single time and unit convention to obtain kinematically consistent acceleration, velocity, and displacement histories [@BooreBommer2005]. The derived acceleration is decomposed by variational mode decomposition (VMD) into $K=10$ modes [@DragomiretskiyZosso2014]; the reconstruction separates the first intrinsic mode (IMF1), whose central frequency is constrained to zero, and retains modes IMF2--IMF10 together with the closure difference of the decomposition.

The model-fitting sample combines the local peak particle acceleration (PPA) and peak particle velocity (PPV) observations from the site monitoring (IMPALA source) with observations from the supplementary S11D source—a database of peak amplitudes measured in different projects worldwide, compiled by the author—admitted only where their distance or MIC lies outside the rectangular distance and charge envelope covered by the local monitoring. Each observation retains its provenance and contributes only to the responses for which it has measurements.

The processed library retains the physical amplitudes of the source time histories. During scenario construction, each component is scaled from its peak particle velocity to the predicted PPV by one factor applied to acceleration, velocity, and displacement. This operation preserves the component's kinematic consistency; transferring temporal shapes among distance--MIC conditions is an explicit analysis assumption.
