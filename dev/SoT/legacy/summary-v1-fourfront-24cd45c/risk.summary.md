The proposed formulation relates blast-induced dynamic demand to the reliability of the critical section of the BRPM tailings storage facility for each scenario defined by source-to-receptor distance, MIC, and predictive amplitude level. Each scenario comprises three-component realizations with common PPV targets and temporal and spectral variability; the plane-strain effective-stress dynamic model will provide the corresponding geotechnical demands.

For each performance criterion, the demand from each realization will be compared with its admissible capacity. The conditional probability of failure will be estimated as the weighted fraction of realizations that exceed the limit state and compared with the approved admissible probability for that criterion; the admissible levels correspond to the geotechnical capacity model. The equivalent reliability index $\beta=-\Phi^{-1}(p_f)$, with $\Phi$ the standard normal distribution function, will express the same comparison on the reliability scale. The criteria will remain separate unless the geotechnical capacity model establishes a joint rule.

This stage defines the evaluation methodology and does not yet provide quantitative risk results. Its application requires the responses of the plane-strain effective-stress dynamic model, the approved capacities and criteria, and the realization weights. Until those inputs are available, neither an acceptability classification nor a scenario-specific probability of failure can be reported.
