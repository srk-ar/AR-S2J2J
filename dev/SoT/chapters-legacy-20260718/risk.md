The risk assessment of the facility under blast-induced demands is the next stage of the program and remains under development. The selected demand histories feed an effective-stress plane-strain dynamic deformation analysis of the critical TSF section, whose response quantities include the excess pore-pressure ratio $r_u$, deformation measures, and the stability or serviceability indicators defined by the project. Pore-pressure generation, cyclic mobility, deformation, and liquefaction-type behaviour are evaluated in that geotechnical model under the corresponding assumptions of material state, drainage, constitutive modelling, and boundary conditions. The results of that assessment are not part of this report and will be documented once the analysis is completed.
