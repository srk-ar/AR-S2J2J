Selection restricts the population of realizations by maximum frequency band, predictive level, and distance and MIC ranges. Within each scenario, the first requested direction defines the record identities compared using the available intensity measures. Selection can rank one or more measures in ascending or descending order, or use random sampling, and retains a declared number of records per scenario.

Once identities have been selected, the requested motion quantities and directions are extracted without modifying the ordinates again. The result is a set of histories identified by record, scenario, direction, and motion quantity. The ranking measure is a demand descriptor; it does not by itself demonstrate that a record is representative or conservative for tailings response.

The current configuration requests H1 acceleration for an 80 Hz band and predictive level 0.90, retains twenty-five records per scenario, and ranks them by descending PGV. This configuration is a particular case of the procedure and requires a scenario family compatible with those filters before selection can run.

Selected identities are related to their frequency band, distance, MIC, predictive level, direction, and intensity measures. This relationship supports both the selected-set index and an inventory of unique records showing the scenarios in which each identity appears. The structure links a subsequent geotechnical response to the history that produced it and to the physical condition that determined its amplitude.

Review visualizations compare selected time histories and response spectra. Their purpose is to inspect the range of demands, repetitions, and contrasts among scenarios; they do not modify histories or replace consistency checks. These products provide traceability and technical review rather than a new motion-generation stage.

Interpretation of the selected set considers peak amplitudes, duration, Arias intensity, CAV, CAV5, and spectral content jointly. Translation of these demands into pore pressure, deformation, and performance belongs to the dynamic model and is reported separately.
