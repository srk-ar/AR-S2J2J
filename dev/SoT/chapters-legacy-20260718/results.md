
The results are separated into model performance, scenario generation, selection, and geotechnical response. Model performance reports the predictive quality of the amplitude model by response, component, and model stage. Scenario generation reports the coverage of the $D$-$Q$ grid, the generated source-record realizations, the scaling factors, and the scenario-history verification checks. Record selection reports the retained histories and the demand descriptors used to rank or compare them. Geotechnical response results report $r_u$, deformation, and project-defined performance measures after the dynamic model is run.

The amplitude-model results begin with the PPA-family and PPV-family errors. PPV results are central because component PPV values define the amplitude targets used to generate the scenario histories and PPV is the conventional blast-vibration amplitude measure. PPA results are reported as the conditional acceleration descriptors of the same records. The multi-response results are reported beside a baseline-versus-conditional comparison on the same response definitions; in the final model, conditioning materially reduced the out-of-fold errors of both amplitude families, with the largest gains in the velocity responses.

Performance is evaluated for the complete training population and separately for the site observations. Restricted to the site, out-of-fold RMSE values in physical units are 2.40 mm/s for resultant PPV and 1.89, 1.62, and 0.76 mm/s for longitudinal, transverse, and vertical PPV. The corresponding PPA values are 0.047 g for the resultant and 0.034, 0.029, and 0.026 g for its components. For the complete training population, the final conditional fit reaches an out-of-fold $R^2$ of 0.85 for resultant PPV, compared with 0.70 when evaluation is restricted to site records. Standard deviations of log-domain residuals range from 0.23 to 0.30.

The scenario-generation results show the realization count, target PPV values, component scale factors, intensity measures, and response spectra for each selected $D$-$Q$ scenario. The audit summary is reported beside these products because it documents the scaling, rotation, and peak behaviour of the generation procedure and identifies the cases requiring review.

The record-selection results define the final demand ensemble passed to the geotechnical analysis. For each selected scenario, the report shows the selected records, their ranking metric, peak amplitudes, cumulative measures, duration measures, and representative spectral content. This lets the later dynamic response results be traced back to the demand realization that produced them.
