**Subject:** BRPM TSF — Blast-Induced Seismic Demand Study: Analysis
Completed and Deliverables

Dear Corné,

We have completed the blast-induced seismic demand analysis for the BRPM
tailings storage facility. This message transmits the study deliverables.

The study provides a robust prediction model of blast-induced seismic demand,
expressed as peak particle velocity and acceleration values (PPV and PPA),
together with a calibrated suite of blast vibration records for the dynamic
deformation model, intended for the analysis of blast-induced dynamic
liquefaction. Predictive performance is supported by out-of-fold evidence,
and each record set retains full traceability to its source measurement and
blasting condition. The scenario-conditioned reliability formulation is
established and will be quantified once the dynamic deformation and
geotechnical capacity models become available.
