## CONTEXT

The document is an English technical report for project AR-S2J2J.R addressing the selection and scaling of measured blasting records for tailings storage facility (TSF) scenario analysis. The purpose of record selection is to represent dynamic demand relevant to saturated fine tailings, with emphasis on pore-pressure accumulation, cyclic mobility, and liquefaction potential. The scope of inquiry is not generic definitions of peak particle velocity, peak particle acceleration, or response spectra, but rather which record descriptors are physically defensible for this specific mechanism and site condition.
