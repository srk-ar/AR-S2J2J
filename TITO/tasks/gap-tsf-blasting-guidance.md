# Gap: TSF and Tailings Guidance for Nearby Blasting Effects

Context:

We are preparing an English technical report for project `AR-S2J2J.R`, which
evaluates the effects of nearby blasting on a TSF. The research gap is not
building damage criteria, human perception, or comfort thresholds. The gap is
which TSF, dam, mining, blasting, or tailings references are relevant to
professional wording about dynamic loading, vibration monitoring,
pore-pressure response, liquefaction screening, and project-specific assessment
for saturated tailings.

Questions:

1. Which authoritative TSF, dam, mining, or tailings guidance documents discuss
   blasting, vibration, seismicity, dynamic loading, pore-pressure monitoring,
   or liquefaction screening as part of TSF safety or performance assessment?
2. Which sources are relevant to a South African mining context, and which are
   only international context or good-practice references?
3. Do the sources provide quantitative acceptance criteria for blasting effects
   on TSFs, or do they require project-specific assessment, monitoring,
   laboratory characterization, and engineering judgement?
4. How should a report distinguish published reference criteria, project
   criteria, monitoring requirements, and analysis assumptions without implying
   that a generic building-vibration criterion governs TSF safety?
5. What source-backed terminology should be used for TSF response to blasting:
   dynamic loading, transient vibration, pore-pressure generation, cyclic
   mobility, liquefaction potential, deformation, stability, monitoring, or
   other terms?
