# Gap: Blast-Vibration Standards, Local Context, and Charge Windows

Context:

We are preparing an English technical report for project `AR-S2J2J.R`, which
evaluates the effects of nearby blasting on a tailings storage facility (TSF)
in a South African mining setting. The research gap is not the basic definition
of PPV, PPA, component peaks, or scaled distance. The gap is which standards,
guidance documents, and charge-window definitions are authoritative enough to
support professional report wording for a TSF/geotechnical blasting assessment
without inventing a compliance criterion.

Questions:

1. Which South African and international standards or guidance documents are
   most relevant for blast-induced ground-vibration monitoring and reporting in
   a mining or TSF context?
2. How do those sources distinguish geotechnical/TSF effects, embankment or
   dam-related performance concerns, structural damage references, and
   monitoring/reporting requirements?
3. How do authoritative blasting references define maximum charge per delay,
   maximum instantaneous charge, and short-delay charge windows?
4. Is a 9 ms charge window supported by standards or literature, or should it be
   treated as a project/vendor-specific definition unless project documents say
   otherwise?
5. How should dual-blast or multi-design events be described when charge and
   distance must be paired by blast design?
