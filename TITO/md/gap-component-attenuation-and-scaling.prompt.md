## CONTEXT

Project `AR-S2J2J.R` evaluates the effects of nearby blasting on a tailings storage facility (TSF). It constructs prediction surfaces over charge and distance for component peak particle velocity (PPV) and component peak particle acceleration (PPA). Recorded blast vibration time histories are then scaled to match predicted component peak accelerations to produce TSF scenario waveform products. The research gap is specific: whether component-wise or multivariate modelling and component-peak scaling have support in blast-vibration literature for geotechnical/TSF input preparation, and how to state the associated limitations accurately. The target output is an English technical report.

## SLOTS

### SLOT 1: Treatment of component PPV/PPA responses in blast-vibration literature

Review how blast-vibration literature treats component PPV and PPA responses: whether they are modelled separately by component (e.g., transverse, vertical, longitudinal), jointly as correlated responses, or summarised through vector or resultant peak quantities. Write in flowing paragraphs.

### SLOT 2: Methods for multivariate or constrained response prediction

Identify the methods and diagnostics used for multivariate or constrained response prediction when blast vibration components are correlated or linked by a vector quantity. Write in flowing paragraphs.

### SLOT 3: Literature precedent for component-peak scaling of recorded time histories

Assess whether the blast-vibration literature contains precedents for scaling recorded blast vibration time histories to match predicted component peak accelerations or velocities. Write in flowing paragraphs.

### SLOT 4: Description of scenario scaling to distinguish from related methods

Determine how scenario scaling of recorded blast time histories to predicted component peaks should be characterised so that it is not confused with spectral matching, target-spectrum adjustment, or synthetic blast waveform simulation. Write in flowing paragraphs.

### SLOT 5: Limitations of peak-component scaling relative to physics-based source models

Identify the limitations that should be stated when scenario waveforms are scaled by peak component response rather than generated from a physics-based blast source model. Write in flowing paragraphs.

## CONSTRAINTS

- The output is written in English.
- Scope is restricted to blast-vibration literature; generic scaled-distance attenuation overviews that do not address the component-wise or multivariate modelling gap are outside scope.
- The subject is component-wise responses (PPV and PPA by axis), not resultant or vector peak quantities alone.
