### Uncertainty Model {#sec-source_uncertainty}

The epistemic uncertainty of the source model is represented through the source logic tree of the adopted regional model, following the standard OpenQuake mechanism: each branch of the tree specifies a complete alternative characterization of the sources — a model composition, or variants of the parameters its authors branched, such as the catalogue declustering method or the seismicity smoothing scheme — with a non-negative weight, and the weights of the set sum to one. The hazard integral is evaluated per branch and the resulting curves are combined with their weights; curve aggregation and quantile extraction follow the OpenQuake implementation [-@OpenQuakeManual2024].

The branching structure belongs to each published regional model. When the source tree of the adopted model consists of a single branch, the epistemic characterization of the sources is the one embedded by its authors in the construction of the model, and the epistemic branching of this assessment concentrates in the ground-motion logic trees ([-@sec-gmm_uncertainty]). The recurrence parameters of each source — the Gutenberg-Richter law with its activity rates and maximum magnitudes — enter the hazard integral through the magnitude and distance densities defined by the source model ([-@eq-hazard-integral]).
