GISTM Seismic Design Criteria by Consequence Class. Source: GISTM [-@gistm2020] . The Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management (GISTM) defines five consequence classes for tailings storage facilities (TSFs), each based on **Population at Risk (PAR)** and the severity of environmental and socio-economic impacts. For all classes, the Maximum Credible Earthquake (MCE) governs if it results in more severe ground motions than the probabilistic criterion. Post-closure criteria are uniform across all classes. GISTM does not use OBE/SEE terminology or dual-level design earthquakes.
